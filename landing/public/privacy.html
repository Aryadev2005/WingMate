Privacy

Last updated October 2026. This covers the early-access waitlist on this website.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and whether you joined the waitlist or reserved the founding price.
  • Who invited you (if you used a friend's link), the country your request came from, and which page section or ad you signed up from.

What we use it for

To email you when Wingmate launches, to honour your place in line and any founding price you reserved, and to understand which of our posts and ads bring people here. We don't sell your email or share it with anyone else.

Advertising measurement

If you arrived from an Instagram or Facebook ad, this site may use the Meta Pixel to tell Meta that a visit or signup happened, so we can measure our ads. You can control this in your Meta ad settings and your browser.

Deleting your data

Reply to any email from us, or write to us, and we'll delete your waitlist entry.

Age

Wingmate is for people aged 18 and over.
